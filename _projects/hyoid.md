The Tuamotu Sandpiper (<i>Prosobonia parvirostris</i>) is a unique species of shorebird native to the Tuamotu Archipelago of French Polynesia. It has been observed to feed on nectar, a behavior that is exceedingly rare for shorebirds. To investigate the morphological precursors that enable this adaptation (and similar dietary habits like biofilm feeding), we took morphological measurements of the hyoid, bill and skull from species across the Scolopacidae family, and compared these with dietary categorizations.
